VERSION 2.2.1 · NOW AVAILABLE

Your bookmark library.
In your language.

Five languages.
Back up when
you choose.

1stTab 2.2.1 is now available, bringing five interface languages and optional, user-started Google Drive backup and restore to your bookmark library.

Update 1stTab from the Chrome Web Store to use these features.

Choose the language that fits

The interface supports English, Russian, Georgian, German, and Japanese. Choose a language in Settings or leave the selection on Automatic to follow your browser language. The choice is stored on your device. Your bookmark titles, folders, notes, tags, and URLs remain your own text.

A backup you start yourself

When you choose to connect Google Drive, 1stTab asks for optional access limited to the app-private Drive folder. A backup sends a versioned export of bookmarks, folders, notes, tags, favorites, and supported appearance and view preferences. Local bookmark management and JSON export/import remain available without connecting an account.

Drive restore shows a preview and adds items; it does not overwrite your existing bookmarks. Review the destination and possible duplicates before confirming. You can delete snapshots in 1stTab or revoke access through Google account settings. Disconnecting does not itself delete backups already stored in Drive.

What this update does not do

Backups are manual. Version 2.2.1 does not schedule backups or continuously sync bookmarks and metadata across devices or Google accounts. Those are separate ideas, not promises of this release.

Privacy and availability

Read the updated Privacy page for what stays local and what is sent when you start a Drive backup. Google authorization is requested only when you choose to connect Drive.
