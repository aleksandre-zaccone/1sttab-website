A LOCAL LIBRARY, FIRST

Your bookmarks are already yours.

1stTab works with your native Chrome bookmarks. The local v2 library needs no account and uploads no bookmark data. Tags, notes, favorites, and preferences stay on this device.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make yourself at home.

Can I install v2 from the Chrome Web Store?

V2 is coming soon. We’re preparing the bookmark-focused release and will share the store link here when it is available. A launch date has not been announced.

Will it use my existing Chrome bookmarks?

Yes. The extension reads and edits your native Chrome bookmarks. You can organize the library you already have, and your changes appear in Chrome’s bookmark manager.

Does v2 sync my notes and tags?

Notes, tags, favorites, and 1stTab settings are local to this device. Native bookmarks may sync through Chrome if you enable Chrome sync. Google Drive sync and sync between Google accounts are planned follow-ups.

What happened to the dashboard features?

We’ve chosen to focus the new 1stTab on bookmark management. Weather, clocks, finance, tasks, and other general dashboard widgets are outside the v2 product. Read the story behind this decision.

Can I keep a backup of my library?

Yes. Export a local JSON copy from Settings → Library tools. Imports show a preview and add a separate Imported folder. Undo and snapshot recovery help with supported changes; exports are useful before extensive edits.
