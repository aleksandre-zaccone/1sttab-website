What's in v1.0 — and what's next

Live features are available right now. Coming soon features are already built and on their way.

v1.0 · Live

A beautiful dashboard
every time you open a tab

1stTab replaces your new tab with a two-column widget layout. Drag widgets between columns, toggle what you need, pick a theme, and set a custom background. Your tab, your way.

  • Drag-and-drop widget layout
  • Light, Dark, and Arcade themes (8 cabinet palettes)
  • Background picker — 6 gradients + upload your own
  • Custom greeting with your name
  • Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave search engine
v1.0 · Live

Smart bookmarks,
front and center

Your most-used sites displayed as a visual grid with favicon icons. Filter instantly, switch between grid and list view, organise into folders, and manage everything from a dedicated Bookmarks Manager page.

  • Grid and list view with folder tabs
  • Live filter — press / to search
  • Pinned strip for top shortcuts
  • Custom favicon icon picker with presets
  • Bookmarks Manager — tree view, drag-reorder, import/export JSON
v1.0 · Live

Your time and
every time zone

Local time is always prominent. Add up to 3 world clocks for teammates or family in any IANA time zone — no app switching needed.

  • Large, clear local time display
  • Up to 3 additional time zones
  • Works across all 8 arcade themes and Light/Dark modes
v1.0 · Live

Weather without
switching tabs

Current conditions and a 5-day forecast pulled from Open-Meteo — no API key, no sign-up. Just set your city and it works.

  • Current temperature and conditions
  • 5-day forecast at a glance
  • Celsius / Fahrenheit toggle
  • Powered by Open-Meteo — free and open-source
