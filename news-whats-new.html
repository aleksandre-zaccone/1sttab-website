A clearer way

1stTab 2.2.2 adds a concise way to see what changed—right inside your bookmark library after an extension update.

The next time you open 1stTab after an extension update, you’ll see the installed version and a short summary of the release. Choose View changes to open the release notes, or dismiss the message and get straight to your bookmarks.

Helpful, not intrusive

The notice appears only after an update, not on first install. It never opens a tab by itself, and dismissing it is remembered for that version. When a later version arrives, its update message can be shown once again.

Your library stays yours

Update notices use a separate local setting. They don’t change bookmarks, folders, tags, notes, favorites, language, or workspace preferences. The message is localized alongside the 1stTab interface in English, Russian, Georgian, German, and Japanese.

Google Drive backup remains optional and user-started. The notice does not connect to Drive or send bookmark data anywhere.
